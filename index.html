Questions

Before you start

Is TenX VIP really free?

Yes, for active Markets Vox traders who opened their account through the TenX link. We check this every week.

I already have a Markets Vox account.

Message TenX Support with your account number. We'll check whether it's linked to TenX and tell you what to do next.

Does TenX ever hold my money?

No. Your funds sit in your own Markets Vox account. TenX staff will never message you first, ask for your password, or ask you to send money so we can manage your account.

How much do I need to start?

Markets Vox has no minimum deposit. Only ever trade with money you can afford to lose, and size every trade for your own account.

Is this financial advice?

No. TenX shares setups and education. Every trade you take is your own decision.
